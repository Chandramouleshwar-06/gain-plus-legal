Delete your Gain+ account

Gain+ is developed by Chandramouleshwar CR.

You can delete your Gain+ account and all data synced to it at any time. You only have an account if you signed in to Gain+ (with Google or with email and password). If you never signed in, all your data is on your phone only and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Gain+ and go to Profile.
  2. In the Account card, tap Delete account and confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes:

  • all your data synced to the cloud (workouts, plans, programs, schedules, exercises, goals, body weight and measurements, food logs, activities, profile and settings),
  • your sign-in (email address and Google account link),
  • the copy of your data on that phone.

If the app asks you to sign in again before removing the sign-in, your synced data has already been deleted. Sign in once more and tap Delete account again to finish.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app, email crchandramouleshwar03@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Gain+ account". Your account and all synced data will be permanently deleted within 30 days, and you will get a confirmation by email.

What is kept

Nothing. No data is kept after your account is deleted. Progress photos are never uploaded, so they stay on your phone until you delete them or uninstall the app.
